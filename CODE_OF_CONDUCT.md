# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
publication of private information, and conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the private channel described in
`SECURITY.md`. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
and comments that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for repeated or serious violations.
