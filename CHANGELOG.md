## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-30

### Added

- Codex v2 RobotExpressive pet with 9 semantic animation rows, 73 populated
  cells, and 16 clockwise look directions.
- Offline WebGL renderer driven by nine original GLB skeletal clips.
- Source-model hash pinning and a machine-readable render/provenance report.
- Strict manifest, atlas-cell, alpha, checksum, and release validation.
- Deterministic `.codex-pet.zip` packaging and SHA-256 release manifest.
- Transactional install, automatic backup/rollback, doctor, and recoverable
  uninstall commands.
- Animated previews, contact sheet, and real-atlas browser laboratory.
- Unit and adversarial tests, coverage gate, CI, Pages, and tagged Release
  workflows.
- English and Chinese setup, acceptance, repair, architecture, research, and
  release documentation.
